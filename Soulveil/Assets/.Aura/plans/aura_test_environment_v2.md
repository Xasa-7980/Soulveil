# Stylized Far-East Village — Aura_Test Scene

## Objective
Build a ~120 × 120 m stylized spring/green far-east village of prefab instances in `AuraScene.unity`: a 28 m central plaza at world `(0, 0, 20)` with the main gateway dead-centre, 6 radiating streets plus a ring street, coherent street-front building clusters, and a naturally framed outer boundary — then Save As `Assets/Scenes/Aura_Test.unity`.

## Changes
- `Assets/Project/00_Prefabs/AuraScene/AuraScene.unity` — (in-memory working scene only) opened and built in via `SaveScene(scene)` → never written on disk.
- `Assets/Scenes/Aura_Test.unity` — (create) final saved scene.
- `Assets/Scenes/Aura_TerrainData.asset` — (create) TerrainData 120 × 120 m, heightmap 513², alphamap 512², 3 FFE terrain layers.
- No `.cs`, `.mat`, `.shader`, `.prefab`, `.asset` inside any pack folder is created or edited. Zero new materials.

## Relevant Assets and Quirks
- Unity `6000.0.58f2`, URP `17.0.4`, Input System `1.14.2`, Cinemachine `3.1.0`. No compile errors.
- **Corrected paths:** `Assets/TriForge` does not exist → `Assets/TriForge Assets/Fantasy Forest Environment/…` (**FFE**). Other three folders exist verbatim.
- **URP-native, so no material work:** `BK_Grass.shader` declares `"RenderPipeline"="UniversalPipeline"`; Far East `M_rocks.mat` uses `_BaseMap/_BaseColor`; FFE `M_FFE_Rock_A.mat` likewise. No magenta risk anywhere.
- **No standalone portal prefab exists in the allowed folders** (searched `portal`/`gate` — only Hovl Studio and `Assets/Project/…/GaiaLobby`, both outside the allowed set). The main gateway is therefore **`M_gatehouse.prefab`** (14.5 × 22.4 × 10.0 m) — the largest true gateway in the pack. Pair with `SM_wall_gate.prefab` (5.36 × 3.98 × 1.92) for the precinct entry.
- `AuraScene.unity` holds exactly 2 roots: `Main Camera` at `(0, 1, -10)` (identity rotation → faces **+Z**) and `Directional Light` at `(0, 3, 0)`. Fog off, ambient skybox, no Volume object; project-wide `Assets/Settings/DefaultVolumeProfile.asset` already applies.
- **Terrain must be centred on the village, not the origin:** Terrain GameObject at `(0, 0, 20)` → covers x ∈ [-60, 60], z ∈ [-40, 80]. A terrain at the origin would clip the village's north half (village reaches z ≈ 62). Camera spawn at z = -10 stays inside.
- **Layout radii measured from village centre VC = (0, 0, 20):** Plaza `r ≤ 14` (28 m dia) · buildings `r 14–34` · fringe `r 34–44` · natural shell `r 44–60` (satisfies "outer 10–15 m natural").
- **Flat-pavement quirk:** all Far East ground pieces are flat and thin — `SM_street_preset_01` 25.91 × **0.21** × 15.90, `SM_road_straight_01` 6.66 × **0.14** × 2.91, `SM_road_corner_01` 6.05 × **0.15** × 6.05. They will clip or float on any slope, so the whole village terrace must be a **single constant elevation**, and roads are laid at `groundY + 0.01` with no bounds snapping.
- **Orientation quirk:** `SM_road_straight_01` runs **along X** (6.66 on X, 2.91 on Z). To align it with a radial at yaw θ it must be rotated to `θ + 90`.
- All Far East prefabs are single-mesh, childless, with `MeshCollider` (verified on `M_gatehouse`, `SM_shrine`, `SM_temple`, `SM_pagoda`, `SM_building_residental_01`, `SM_street_preset_01`, `SM_road_straight_01`) — bounds anchoring is reliable for them. Many **FFE** prefabs report "bounds: unavailable", so Y-snapping must aggregate `GetComponentsInChildren<Renderer>()` bounds, never `transform.position.y`.
- **Excluded:** all FFE `_Autumn` / `_Winter` / `_Billboard` prefabs; Far East `(1)`/`(2)`/`(3)` duplicates; BK Islands `IslandCliff*_Sand` / `*_underwater`; BK Meadows `Mountains/Mountain1|2.prefab` (bounds report ~0.03 m — unusable).
- **Scale coherence:** Far East architecture 6–30 m and FFE trees 8–20 m agree. BK Meadows `Oak1–6` (12–27 m) and `Elm1–6` would dwarf the 11 m residential buildings — so BK Meadows contributes only `Birch1/2`, `Cypres1`, `Bush1–3`, ground plants and small props.
- `SM_shrine_lanterns.prefab` carries a stray `TMP_InputField` — instantiate as-is, never edit the prefab.
- Imports required in `execute_code`: `UnityEngine`, `UnityEditor`, `UnityEditor.SceneManagement`, `System.Collections.Generic`.

### Deviations

- **Roads:** 3-4 `SM_road_straight_01` per radial at **scale 1.5** (~4.4 m wide x 10 m long) instead of 5 pieces at 6.6 m spacing. At scale 1.0 the piece is only 2.9 m wide (a path, not a street), and 4 pieces at that width would have crossed the r=46 edge of the flat terrace. The planned r=30 ring street was replaced by 6 tangential connectors at r=25 plus 2 perpendicular spurs, because a ring there would have cut straight through the street-front houses.
- **Pagoda:** placed on-axis at `(0, *, 54)` on the raised precinct platform rather than `(0, *, 62)`, so it terminates the axial view *through* the gateway. `SM_temple` became the off-axis east landmark instead.
- **Market square:** moved from r=20 on the 240 deg radial to the east fringe pocket at `(42, *, 20)`. On a 6-radial plan every small-radius wedge is occupied once the 9 m street offset is applied; the new pocket was confirmed clear of all buildings.
- **North radial** shortened to 2 pieces so the precinct gate frontage sits on the road instead of being overrun by it. 4 mis-aligned road pieces were deleted before re-placing.
- **Rocks:** 23 placed rather than the nominal 30. The rejection test that keeps rocks out of buildings, roads and the axial corridor rejected about a third of the candidates — the intended behaviour.
- **Gateway orientation (verified, not guessed):** raycasting through the `M_gatehouse` collider confirmed a 3.25 m-wide passage running along **Z** at x=0, open from ground to 16 m. The player at (0,1,-10) therefore looks straight through the portal to the pagoda. No rotation correction was needed.
- **Material side-effect, reverted:** instantiating the cliff and banner prefabs caused Unity to re-serialize `M_cliffs.mat` and `M_bannersFarEast 1.mat` (filling a legacy `_MainTex` slot) during the save — both files carry a 02:53:52 mtime, the same second as `SaveScene`. Each diff was exactly one line, so both were restored to their committed state with `git checkout`. The re-save that applied the post-verification camera fix re-triggered the same benign normalisation; both were reverted again and **the final `git status` lists neither material**.
- **Stray artifact, deleted:** a camera self-test wrote `Assets/Screenshots/`; that folder and its `.meta` were removed, so the only new assets are `Aura_Test.unity` and `Aura_TerrainData.asset`.
- **Source scene proven untouched:** `Assets/Project/00_Prefabs/AuraScene/AuraScene.unity` is still **8168 bytes** with mtime **02:27:27**, i.e. unchanged from before the save at 02:53. Only the path-overload `SaveScene(scene, path)` was ever called.

### Post-verification fix

The end-of-run verification pass returned `VERDICT: partial` and caught one real defect: `Main Camera` was still at its original `y = 1`, which is **1.8 m below the 2.80 m terrace**, so the ground plane cut the player view and the gateway passage was occluded. The other checks passed (source untouched, clean build, hierarchy, composition, axial/portal all pass; git-status and boundary checks were reported untested because that agent had no shell or camera tool available).

- Raised `Main Camera` to `(0, 4.40, -10)` — terrace 2.80 + 1.6 m eye height — keeping identity rotation, restoring the axial view down the street and through the portal. Confirmed by a play-mode game-view capture: gateway centred with `SM_pagoda` visible *through* its arch, street fronts lining both sides, open plaza, no magenta.
- Relocated the 3 props that had landed on the plaza paving (`SM_streetlamp_03`, `SM_sign_shop_01`, `SM_banner_01`), pushed outward to radius 16–18.
- Re-saved `Assets/Scenes/Aura_Test.unity`. **No geometry was moved to fix the camera** — the correction is a single scene-instance Transform change.
